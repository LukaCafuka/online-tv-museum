I got this timecode generator from Petrinja. A man called me after seeing my ads that I was looking for old broadcast/AV equipment, and he told me he had two interesting pieces of equipment for sale. One of them was the FOR.A TGR-3300. I cannot find information online related to this specific model. <br><br>

This generator supports LTC (Linear Timecode) and VITC (Vertical Interval Timecode) generation and reading. It has a very intuitive front panel interface for time configuration and for starting/stopping the timecode. It also supports superimposed visual timecode counter through one of the video outputs, which makes it even easier to use.
