I found this VCR for sale completely by accident while searching for U-matic terms on Google Images, and I came across a Njuškalo listing that had expired back in 2021. <br> <br>
I called the seller, and by some miracle the unit was still available. On top of that, the seller told me he had a few [other VCRs](/equipment/jvc-hr-d158ms/) he could show me and possibly sell. I met with him, and while testing the unit with a recorded tape, a problem appeared. The unit showed noise even though there was a recording on the tape. Despite that, I bought the VCR in a lot together with the other machines he offered and took it home. After inspecting it at home, the unit was in okay condition; cobwebs had built up in some areas, and after a bit of cleaning everything inside looked fine. Once I cleaned the helical drum, the unit worked completely normally :D <br> <br>
This VCR comes from the Goethe-Institut in Zagreb. It is unknown what it was used for, but considering I got two U-matic tapes with recorded western movies along with it, maybe it was literally used only for watching entertainment programs? <br><br>
This particular U-matic model is special because it is a multi-standard version. It can record and play back PAL, SECAM, and NTSC 443 video standards.
The unit works flawlessly, which is fascinating because it was probably serviced a long time ago. Maybe it was never serviced at all. <br>
The only issue the unit currently has is that the analog counter on the front does not work during playback; it stays stuck in its initial position. The counter only moves when the tape is rewound.

Pronašao sam ovaj magnetoskop na prodaji skroz slučajno kada sam pretraživao U-matic riječi u Google slikama i pronašao sam Njuškalo oglas koji je istekao u 2021. <br> <br>
Zvao sam prodavača i nekim čudom uređaj je još uvijek bio dostupan. Uz to, prodavač mi je rekao da ima još par [ostalih magnetoskopa](/oprema/jvc-hr-d158ms/) koje mi može pokazati i eventualno prodati. Našao sam se sa prodavačem te pri testiranju uređaja sa snimljenom kasetom, došlo je do problema. Uređaj je prikazivao snijeg iako je postajala snimka na kaseti. Unatoč tome, kupio sam magnetoskop u lotu sa ostalim magnetoskopima koje je nudio te sam ga odnio doma. Nakon inspekcije doma, uređaj je bio u oke stanju, paučina se ulovila u nekim dijelovima i nakon malo čišćenja bilo je sve uredno unutra. Kada sam očistio helikoidni bubanj, uređaj je radio sasvim normalno :D <br> <br>
Magnetoskop dolazi iz Goethe instituta u Zagrebu. Nepoznato je za što se magnetoskop koristio, no sudeći da sam uz magnetoskop dobio i dve U-matic kasete sa snimljenim vestern filmovima, možda se doslovno koristilo samo za gledanje zabavnih programa? <br><br>
Konkretno ovaj U-matic model je poseban po tome što je multi-standard verzija. Može snimati i reproducirati PAL, SECAM i NTSC 443 video standarde.
Uređaj radi besprijekorno što je fascinantno jer je vjerojatno davno servisiran. Možda čak i nikad nije servisiran. <br>
Jedini problem koji uređaj trenutačno ima je da mu ne radi analogni brojač naprijed kada se pušta kaseta, ostaje zaglavljena u prvobitnoj poziciji. Jedino kada se kaseta premotava unazad se brojač okreće.
