I'd been looking for this VCR for about two years. It's hard to find one for sale at a reasonable price because they're rare and in very high demand for digitizing VHS and S-VHS. <br> <br>
I found a listing in April 2026 on kleinanzeigen.de. The seller claimed he had tested the unit with a tape and that it worked (except for the video output—that hadn't been tested). We agreed on a reasonable price and he shipped it to Croatia by post. When the unit arrived, it was faulty… At first the power supply didn't work at all - the unit wouldn't power on. It turned out several solder joints on the PSU board had cracked and needed to be repaired. After repairing the power supply, the unit powered on; I inserted a tape and ran into more problems… The take-up reel that winds the magnetic tape during playback wasn't spinning. When I looked deeper into the machine, it turned out the main board had cracked in two places and severed the connection to the servo boards that control the mechanism. My impression is that the unit was probably thrown around several times in transit. Although it looked fine on the outside, the inside wasn't in good shape. Once the connections on the main board were repaired, the unit finally worked properly again. <br> <br>

This is currently the best VCR I have for digitizing VHS and S-VHS tapes. To me it gives the sharpest and most clearest picture. It comes a bit close to vhsdecode quality. In EDIT mode the picture is sharp and color transitions are cleaner than on the other VCRs in my collection. The built-in frame TBC is also very useful, but I think Panasonic DMR DVD recorders are still better at stabilizing the picture than this Sony; the built-in frame TBC is still perfectly adequate for digitizing tapes with a normal signal. <br> Unfortunately component video isn't available unless the optional SVBK-170 PCB board is installed (which I didn't get with the unit). <br><br>
The unit also has a very good maintenance menu that lets you adjust parameters that on other VCRs require a screwdriver and opening the chassis.
<br><br>
In my personal opinion, this unit is ideal for digitizing SP VHS and S-VHS tapes; so far I haven't seen anything better than it. (Except of course vhsdecode, but that's a completely different approach to digitization.)

Ovaj magnetoskop sam tražio već oko dvije godine. Teško ga je pronaći u prodaji po razumnoj cijeni jer su rijetki i izuzetno traženi za digitalizaciju VHS-a i S-VHS-a. <br> <br>
Pronašao sam oglas u travnju 2026. na kleinanzeigen.de. Prodavač je tvrdio da je uređaj isprobao s kasetom i da radi (osim video izlaza — to nije bilo testirano). Dogovorili smo razumnu cijenu i poslao mi ga je poštom u Hrvatsku. Kad je uređaj stigao, bio je u neispravnom stanju… Na prvi pogled, napajanje uopće nije radilo — uređaj se nije palio. Ispostavilo se da je na ploči napajanja puklo nekoliko lemova, pa su morali biti popravljeni. Nakon popravka napajanja, uređaj se upalio; ubacio sam kasetu i opet problemi… Prijemni kolut koji namata magnetsku vrpcu kad se pušta reprodukcija nije se okretao. Kad sam malo dublje zaviruo u stroj, ispostavilo se da je matična ploča na dva mjesta pukla i prekinula vezu s servo pločama koje upravljaju mehanizmom. Moj je dojam da je uređaj tijekom dostave vjerojatno nekoliko puta grubo bačen. Iako je izvana izgledao u redu, unutrašnje stanje nije bilo dobro. Kad su popravljeni spojevi na matičnoj ploči, uređaj je napokon u potpunosti proradio. <br> <br>

Ovaj je uređaj trenutačno najbolji magnetoskop za digitalizaciju VHS i S-VHS kaseta kojeg imam. Meni se čini da daje najoštru i najvjerniju sliku. Približava se kvaliteti vhsdecode-a. U EDIT načinu rada slika je oštra, a prijelazi boja čišći su nego na drugim magnetoskopima iz moje kolekcije. Ugrađeni frame TBC je također jako koristan, ali mislim da su Panasonic DMR DVD rekorderi i dalje bolji u stabilizaciji slike od ovog Sonyja; ugrađeni frame TBC ipak je sasvim u redu za digitalizaciju kaseta s normalnim signalom. <br> Nažalost, komponentni video nije dostupan osim ako nije ugrađena dodatna SVBK-170 PCB ploča (koju ja nisam dobio uz uređaj). <br><br>
Uređaj također ima vrlo dobar servisni („maintenance”) meni kojim se može podešavati ono što bi na drugim magnetoskopima trebalo raditi šrafcigerom i otvaranjem kućišta.
<br><br>
Po mom osobnom mišljenju, ovaj je uređaj idealan za digitalizaciju SP VHS-a i S-VHS kaseta; zasad nisam vidio ništa bolje od njega. (Osim naravno vhsdecode-a, ali to je potpuno drugačiji pristup digitalizaciji.)
