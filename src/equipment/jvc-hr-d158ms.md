Very strange and somewhat rare multistandard JVC VHS VTR. The VTR comes from the Goethe-Institut in Zagreb. It is unknown what it was used for there. <br><br>

I found this VTR for sale completely by accident while searching for U-matic terms in Google Images, and I came across a Njuškalo listing that had expired in 2021.
I called the seller, who mentioned that the [U-matic VTR](/equipment/sony-vo5630/) was still for sale and that he also had other VTRs, one of which was the **JVC HR-D158MS**. <br> When I met with the seller, we tested the unit and it turned out to be fully functional. In the end, I took it as part of a lot with the other VTRs he was selling. <br><br>

This VTR supports PAL, SECAM, and NTSC playback. I am not sure whether it can record all of those standards, because recording is available only through the RF input on the TV tuner. Luckily, I managed to push myself to test PAL recording using a fake RF channel and two antennas. PAL recording works completely normally.

Jako čudan i pomalo rijedak multistandardni JVC VHS magnetoskop. Magnetoskop dolazi iz Goethe instituta u Zagrebu. Nepoznato je za što se koristio tamo. <br><br>

Pronašao sam ovaj magnetoskop na prodaji skroz slučajno kada sam pretraživao U-matic riječi u Google slikama i pronašao sam Njuškalo oglas koji je istekao u 2021.
Zvao sam prodavača koji mi je spomenuo da je [U-matic magnetoskop](/oprema/sony-vo5630/) još uvjek na prodaji te da ima još ostalih magnetoskopa, jedan od njih je bio **JVC HR-D158MS**. <br> Kada sam se našao sa prodavačem, uređaj smo testirali i ispada da je bio skroz funkcionalan. Na kraju sam ga uzeo u lotu sa ostalim magnetoskopima koje je prodavao. <br><br>

Magnetoskop podržava PAL, SECAM i NTSC reprodukciju. Nisam siguran ako može snimati sve navedene standarde jer je snimanje dostupno samo preko RF ulaza na TV tuneru. Svom srećom, uspio sam se natjerati i testirati PAL snimanje sa lažnim RF kanalom i dve antene. PAL snimanje radi sasvim normalno.
