I had been looking for this JVC S-VHS VTR for a long time. They're very rare because very few people used S-VHS in a professional environment (the S-VHS format wasn't very reliable). On top of that, the deck is very large and heavy, so most people don't want to ship it. I managed to find this one at an eBay auction for €200; the seller couldn't sell it because he hadn't enabled the shipping option, so it wasn't showing up in listing searches. I managed to negotiate the price down a bit and we agreed that he would ship it to me by post. <br><br>
When the VTR arrived, there were power supply problems. At first it would only power on for half a minute (which is too long), and when I inserted my first test tape it shut off and wouldn't power on again, and the tape got stuck inside. It turned out the power supply board had cracked and severed the contacts. After repairing the power supply, the VTR worked perfectly. <br><br>
Unfortunately I didn't get the optional TBC boards and timecode boards. It came in the base configuration. The unit works but has ghosting and oversharpening issues, so it's not reliable for digitizing. In my opinion, the best thing about this VCR is that it allows forward and reverse shuttle speeds up to 32 times with a visible picture. That lets me quickly preview content on S-VHS and VHS tapes. <br>
As far as I know, this is the only professional S-VHS VCR that supports compact (S-VHS-C and VHS-C) cassettes without an adapter. <br><br>
Personally, this is my favorite S-VHS/VHS VCR series ever made. I love their professional look and Betacam-style VTR design.

Ovaj JVC S-VHS magnetoskop sam tražio već dugo, jako su rijetki jer je malo tko koristio S-VHS u profesionalnom okruženju (S-VHS format nije bio baš pouzdan). Uz to, skop je jako velik i težak pa ga malo tko hoće slati poštom. Uspio sam ovaj naći na aukciji na Ebayu za 200€, prodavač nikako da ga proda jer nije stavio opciju za dostavu pa se nije prikazvao na pretragama oglasa. Uspio sam malo smanjiti cijenu i dogovorili smo se da mi je pošalje poštom do mene. <br><br>
Kada je magnetoskop stigao do mene, bilo je problema sa napajanjem, na prvu se palio svega pola minute (što je predugo) a kada sam ubacio svoju prvu kasetu za test - izgasio se i više se nije mogao upaliti a kaseta se zaglavila unutra. Ispada da je ploča za napajanje puknula i presjekla kontakte, nakon popravka napajanja, magnetoskop se savršeno radio. <br><br>
Nažalost nisam dobio opcionalne TBC ploče i timecode ploče, došao je u najslabijoj konfiguraciji. Uređaj radi no ima <i>ghosting</i> i <i>oversharpening</i> problema stoga nije pouzdan za presnimavanje. Po mom mišljenju, najbolja stvar kod ovog magnetoskopa je što omogućuje premotavanje naprijed i nazad do 32x brzine sa vidljivom slikom. To mi omogučava brz pregled sadržaja sa S-VHS i VHS kaseta. <br>
Koliko ja znam, ovo je jedini profesionalan S-VHS magnetoskop koji podržava i kompaktne (S-VHS-C i VHS-C) kasete bez adaptera. <br><br>
Ovo je meni osobno najdraža S-VHS/VHS magnetoskop serija ikada napravljena, obožavam njihov profesionalan izgled i dizajn nalik betacam magnetoskopima.
